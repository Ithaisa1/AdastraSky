# Teide Observatory (OT)

The Teide Observatory is located in Izaña, Tenerife, at an altitude of 2,390 metres, within the
Teide National Park. It is one of the most important solar observatories in the world.

## Astronomical characteristics

- Seeing: 0.6–1.0 arcseconds
- Clear nights: >70% per year
- Bortle Scale: 2 (excellent sky)
- Altitude: 2,390 m
- Coordinates: 28.30°N, 16.51°W

## Facilities

The OT houses solar and nocturnal telescopes from multiple countries, specialising in
solar physics due to the exceptional atmospheric transparency conditions.

## Main telescopes

- GREGOR: 1.5 m, the largest solar telescope in Europe
- THEMIS: 90 cm, Franco-Italian solar telescope
- Carlos Sánchez Telescope: 1.5 m
- IAC-80: 80 cm
- VTT: 70 cm, German solar telescope
- OGS: 1 m, ESA telescope

## Research

Main research areas include: solar physics, helioseismology, stellar astrophysics,
atmospheric physics, and light pollution studies.

## Visits

The observatory can be visited by prior appointment. The visitor centre is in Izaña, accessible
via the TF-24 road. Warm clothing is recommended even in summer.
