# Astronomical Events Visible from the Canary Islands

The Canary Islands offer privileged conditions for observing astronomical
events throughout the year.

## Meteor showers

| Event | Peak date | Rate (ZHR) |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| Quadrantids | 3–4 January | 120 meteors/hour |
| Lyrids | 22–23 April | 18 meteors/hour |
| Perseids | 12–13 August | 100 meteors/hour |
| Orionids | 21–22 October | 20 meteors/hour |
| Geminids | 13–14 December | 150 meteors/hour (best of the year) |

## Eclipses

- Partial solar eclipse: visible from the Canary Islands with magnitude varying by island
- Total lunar eclipse: fully visible from the entire archipelago

## Visible planets

Mars, Jupiter and Saturn are visible to the naked eye during most of the year from the
Canary Islands. Venus is especially bright at dusk or dawn. Mercury is visible
in short periods near the horizon.

## Best time for observation

- Summer: longer nights and pleasant temperatures at altitude. Ideal for the Milky Way.
- Winter: more transparent skies. Better seeing. Shorter days.
- Spring and autumn: good balance between temperature and hours of darkness.
