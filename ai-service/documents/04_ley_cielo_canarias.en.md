# Canary Islands Sky Law and Astronomical Protection

Law 31/1988, of 31 October, on the protection of the astronomical quality of the
Observatories of the Instituto de Astrofísica de Canarias, known as the "Sky Law",
is pioneering legislation worldwide.

## Main provisions

1. **Lighting control**: Regulates the types of luminaires, their orientation and the
   light intensity permitted in areas near the observatories.

2. **Radio restrictions**: Limits radio frequency emissions that may
   interfere with astronomical observations.

3. **Protection zones**: Defines a protection perimeter around the observatories
   where restrictions are stricter.

4. **Impact assessment**: Requires astronomical impact studies for any project
   that may affect sky quality.

## Starlight Reserve

The Canary Islands were declared a Starlight Reserve in 2014 by the Starlight Foundation, a recognition
of the quality of its skies and its commitment to protecting the firmament. This boosted
astrotourism as a sustainable economic activity in the islands.

## Penalties

Infringements of the Sky Law can lead to significant fines and the obligation to
correct installations that do not comply with regulations.
