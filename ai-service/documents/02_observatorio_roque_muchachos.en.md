# Roque de los Muchachos Observatory (ORM)

The Roque de los Muchachos Observatory (ORM) is located in the municipality of Garafía on the
island of La Palma, at an altitude of 2,396 metres. It is one of the best locations in the northern
hemisphere for astronomical observation due to the exceptional quality of its sky.

## Astronomical characteristics

- Seeing: 0.4–0.8 arcseconds (excellent)
- Clear nights: >75% per year
- Bortle Scale: 1 (pristine sky)
- Altitude: 2,396 m
- Coordinates: 28.76°N, 17.88°W

## Main telescopes

- Gran Telescopio Canarias (GTC): 10.4 m, the world's largest optical telescope
- William Herschel Telescope (WHT): 4.2 m
- Telescopio Nazionale Galileo (TNG): 3.58 m
- Nordic Optical Telescope (NOT): 2.56 m
- Isaac Newton Telescope (INT): 2.5 m
- Liverpool Telescope: 2.0 m (robotic)
- MAGIC: Cherenkov telescopes for gamma rays
- LST-1: large-sized Cherenkov telescope

## Visits

The observatory offers guided tours by prior reservation. The visitor centre is located
in the GTC building. It is recommended to visit at sunset to enjoy the views and the
sunset over the Atlantic Ocean.
