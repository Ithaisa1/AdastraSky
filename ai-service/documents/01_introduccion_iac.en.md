# Instituto de Astrofísica de Canarias (IAC)

The Instituto de Astrofísica de Canarias (IAC) is a Spanish research centre founded in
1975, headquartered in San Cristóbal de La Laguna, Tenerife. It is the body responsible for
managing the Canary Islands Observatories: the Roque de los Muchachos Observatory (ORM) on La Palma
and the Teide Observatory (OT) on Tenerife.

## Mission

The IAC's mission is to promote, coordinate and develop research in astrophysics and
space sciences, as well as scientific outreach and the training of research personnel.
It is considered one of the most important astrophysics institutes in Europe.

## Infrastructure

The skies of the Canary Islands are protected by Law 31/1988 of the Sky, which regulates light
pollution and radio emissions to guarantee astronomical quality. The Canary
Islands hold the "Starlight Reserve" designation by UNESCO.

## Key facts

- Headquarters: La Laguna, Tenerife
- Observatories: ORM (La Palma) and OT (Tenerife)
- Telescopes operated: over 20 international instruments
- Staff: more than 300 researchers and technicians
- Founded: 1975
