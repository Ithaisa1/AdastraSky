# Astrotourism in the Canary Islands

Astrotourism is a growing tourist activity in the Canary Islands, taking advantage of the exceptional
quality of its skies. All islands offer astronomical observation experiences.

## Best places by island

### Tenerife
- Teide Observatory (Izaña): guided tours and solar observation
- Teide National Park: astronomical viewpoints at 2,000+ m
- Mirador de Las Minas de San José: spectacular views of the caldera

### La Palma
- Roque de los Muchachos Observatory: the best sky in Europe
- Mirador de Llano del Jable: excellent for astrophotography
- Caldera de Taburiente National Park

### La Gomera
- Mirador de Abrante: dark sky over the sea of clouds
- Garajonay National Park: ancient forest and starry skies

### El Hierro
- Mirador de Las Playas: minimal light pollution
- Montaña de Tenerra: exceptional altitude and darkness

### Gran Canaria
- Degollada de La Yegua: astronomical viewpoint on the summit
- Pico de Las Nieves: the highest point on the island

### Lanzarote
- Mirador del Río: dark sky with views of the Chinijo Archipelago
- Timanfaya National Park: volcanic landscapes and stars

### Fuerteventura
- Cueva de Lobos: dark beach ideal for astrophotography
- Betancuria: mountainous interior with low light pollution

## Recommendations

- Check the moon phase before planning an observation
- Bring warm clothing (night temperatures drop significantly at altitude)
- Use red-light flashlights to preserve night vision adaptation
- Book guided tours in advance at the observatories
